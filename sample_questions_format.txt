[Section: General Aptitude]

Q1. If 25% of a number is 40, what is the number?

A) 100
B) 120
C) 160
D) 200
Ans: C
Exp: 25% of N = 40, so N = 40 / 0.25 = 160.

[Section: Electrical Engineering]

Q2. In DC steady state, an ideal inductor behaves as:

A) Open circuit
B) Short circuit
C) Current source
D) Voltage source
Ans: B
Exp: For steady DC, di/dt = 0, so the ideal inductor voltage is zero.
